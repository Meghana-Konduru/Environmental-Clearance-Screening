## Metadata Requirements

For every document, maintain:

- document_id
- document_name
- document_type
- issuing_authority
- document_number
- issue_date
- source_url
- retrieval_date
- applicability_status

## Usage

These documents support regulatory retrieval and screening.

Historical or withdrawn documents must not be treated as
currently applicable regulatory requirements.

All regulatory conclusions must be supported by the
applicable notification, amendments and official documents.
